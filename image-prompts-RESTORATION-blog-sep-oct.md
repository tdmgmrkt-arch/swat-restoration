# S.W.A.T. Restoration — Blog Hero Prompts (Aug 28 – Oct 19 batch, 10 posts)

> **House style — derived from the published set in `public/blog/`.**
>
> - **No people in frame.** No technicians, no hands, no homeowners.
> - **No branding.** No truck, no logo, no uniform, no shield overlay.
> - **16:9 landscape**, 1600px wide.
> - **Flat, natural light** — overcast daylight or soft interior daylight.
>   No dramatic lighting, no glow, no teal-orange grading, no lens flare.
>   The site overlay supplies the navy/tactical mood; the photo stays clean.
> - **Room-scale where the damage is room-scale** (a stained ceiling in a
>   lived-in living room), tighter where the subject is a component.
> - **Real DFW residential context** — brick ranch homes, beige walls,
>   hardwood and tile, builder-grade interiors, dormant Bermuda outside.
> - Subject reads at a glance, with negative space for the headline overlay.
>
> **Pipeline:** generate → drop in `public/blog/` → convert. The converter
> skips the legacy migration originals automatically, but the safest form is
> to name the files explicitly:
>
> ```
> node scripts/convert-blog-pngs.mjs <file1> <file2> ...
> ```

**Filenames must match the post slug exactly** — this repo names heroes
`/blog/<full-slug>.webp`, unlike the plumbing site's shortened names.
