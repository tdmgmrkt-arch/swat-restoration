How to Prepare Your Home for Storm Season

Storm season in Fort Worth, TX, can bring heavy rains, strong winds, and even hail, putting homes at risk of flooding and structural damage. The best way to protect your property is through proactive preparation.

Start by inspecting your roof for loose shingles and weak spots—these are often the first areas to sustain damage. Clean out your gutters to ensure proper drainage and prevent water from backing up into your home. If your area is prone to flooding, consider installing a sump pump or using sandbags to create a barrier around entry points.
