Common Types of Storm Damage

Storms in our area can hit hard and leave behind different kinds of damage. Some of the most common include:

  • Roof Damage from Hail and Wind – Shingles can crack, curl, or get ripped away, leaving your roof vulnerable to leaks.
  • Water Intrusion – Heavy rain or flooding can cause water to seep into your foundation, basement, or walls.
  • Broken Windows and Siding – High winds and flying debris can shatter glass or damage exterior walls.
  • Fallen Trees and Debris – Large branches can collapse onto roofs, fences, or vehicles.

These problems don’t just affect your home’s appearance—they compromise safety and can lead to long-term issues like mold growth or structural instability if not addressed right away.

Why Fast Response Matters

The first 24–48 hours after a storm are critical. Water that isn’t dried immediately can cause mold. A damaged roof left exposed can lead to major leaks. And insurance claims often require quick documentation of damage.
